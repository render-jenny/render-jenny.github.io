# Changelog

## 2026-11-18: underwater sound (not yet live)

- A "Sound" button beside the pause button, off until pressed. It plays a calm, endless soundtrack made in the browser: a soft pad of slow chords, water swells, a few bubbles and a sparse soft melody, under a long echo. It gets more muffled as you scroll deeper.
- Bubbles come in two sizes (small and high, big and low) and sink lower as you scroll deeper; a sparse melody of single notes in a warm electric-piano tone plays every 8 to 16 seconds, and about 4 in 10 notes have a faint echo.
- Quiet bubble sounds on button presses and when a demo opens or closes (only while sound is on).
- No audio files and nothing from other websites. Spec: [specs/02-sound.md](specs/02-sound.md).

- Portfolio page: a sunlit sea that gets darker as you scroll, with pixel fish and a poke-able jellyfish, in light and dark.
