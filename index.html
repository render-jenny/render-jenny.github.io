A 90-second walk-through of the live app, recorded with made-up demo accounts. The video has no sound, so the steps are written out below it.

What happens in the video
  1. 0:00 Sign up with an email address and a password.
  2. 0:12 Browse the press feed and search for "jacket".
  3. 0:16 Save an article to a new list called "Jacket press".
  4. 0:32 Open the list and add a private note to the article.
  5. 0:51 The same app on a phone: log in.
  6. 1:03 See your private lists.
  7. 1:15 Save an article to a list from the phone.
  8. 1:25 The account page, where a member can delete their account and everything in it.

Bootcamp Connect: demo

A walk-through of the working prototype, under two minutes. The members, posts and chats are made-up demo data. Accounts are saved only in the browser, because there is no server yet. The video has no sound, so the steps are written out below it.

What happens in the video
  1. 0:01 Sign up with an email address and choose the Software Developer track.
  2. 0:17 Set up a profile with a headline, an About section and skills, then save it.
  3. 0:42 The feed, filtered to paid and unpaid opportunities.
  4. 0:50 Open an opportunity and read the pay, dates and responsibilities.
  5. 0:57 Apply with a short message. In the demo, the person who posted it accepts and a project chat opens.
  6. 1:07 Connect: accept an invitation, then send a connection request with a note.
  7. 1:22 Messages: write in a project group chat and get a reply.
  8. 1:40 The finished profile page.
